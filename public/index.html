How it works on each device
  • Computer: drag to look, WASD to move, click a booth wall, a board, a model, or a PDF to leave a note.
  • Phone: drag or turn your phone to look, tap the floor to walk, tap a surface to leave a note.
  • Quest / headset: press VR, point and pull the trigger. Left stick walks, right stick turns, A or X toggles your mic.
  • Cardboard: press VR, aim the ring with your head, and press the Cardboard button to walk to the floor spot, open a note, or press a button.
  • Fly: once inside, press Fly to float freely through the hall and over models.

Bringing in a Revit model or drawings
  • 3D model: in Revit, open a 3D view and use File > Export > FBX. Open the FBX in Blender (free) and use File > Export > glTF 2.0, format glTF Binary (.glb). A Revit glTF exporter add-in works too.
  • Keep it light: hide furniture, site, and fine detail you do not need. Files under about 30 MB load well on phones and headsets.
  • Drawings and sheets: print or export them to PDF. Everyone sees the same page, and notes stick to the page they were placed on.
  • The model appears in front of you. Use Objects to move, turn, resize, or switch between real scale and fit-to-room.
